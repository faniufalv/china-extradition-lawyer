## Q1: [Intellectual Property] For Expatriates: What is the statute of limitations for labor arbitration?

**A**: According to Article 27 of the Labor Dispute Mediation and Arbitration Law, the limitation period is one year from when the party knew or should have known their rights were infringed. For wage disputes during ongoing employment, the one-year limit does not apply; but after termination, claims must be filed within one year.

**法律依据**: 《劳动合同法》

**领域**: 劳动法 | **语言**: English

## Q2: [Criminal Defense] What is insider trading crime and how to defend against it?

**A**: Insider trading crime refers to insiders or those illegally obtaining inside information trading securities before information is public. Defense points: 1) Whether information qualifies as statutory inside information; 2) Whether defendant is statutory insider; 3) Causal relationship between trading and inside information.

**法律依据**: 《中华人民共和国刑法》第180条

**领域**: 证券犯罪 | **语言**: English
